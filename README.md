# The Anti-Slop Kit

**Free AI skills for presentations, social posts and email that don't look or sound like AI made them. By [Social Mode](https://socialmode.com).**

Everyone has the same tools now, so everything is starting to look the same, from the cream backgrounds and elegant serifs to the captions that open with "In today's fast-paced world" and close with "Let that sink in." People have learnt to scroll straight past it.

This kit is the set of rules I use to stop that happening. They came out of real client work and a long list of things I kept having to fix by hand. I'm giving them away because the brands that win the next few years won't be the ones making the most content. They'll be the ones with taste.

Jessica Valentine, Social Mode

## What's inside

| Skill | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `anti-slop-writing` | The core. Rhythm, the phrases that give AI away, specificity and point of view. The other three build on it. |
| `anti-slop-social` | Instagram and LinkedIn carousels, covers, captions and bios. Layout, type and design rules as well as copy. |
| `anti-slop-presentations` | Pitch decks, proposals and strategy decks that make an argument instead of filling slides. |
| `anti-slop-email` | Outreach, follow-ups, client updates and newsletters that read like a busy person wrote them. |

Each skill can also **audit** a draft: ask "does this sound like AI?" and it will quote the lines that give it away and suggest fixes, without rewriting your work.

## How to use it

### In Claude

1. Download the zip for the skill you want from the [`dist`](dist) folder, or `anti-slop-kit-all-skills.zip` for all four.
2. In Claude, go to Settings, find Skills, and upload the zip. Upload each skill zip separately.
3. Ask for a post, deck or email as normal. Claude will pick up the skill when it's relevant, or you can say "use the anti-slop social skill".

In Claude Code, copy the folders from [`skills`](skills) into `~/.claude/skills/`.

### In ChatGPT, Gemini or anything else

Open the [`prompts`](prompts) folder, copy the file you need and paste it into ChatGPT's Custom Instructions or a Project, a Gemini Gem, or the instructions box of whichever tool you use. Each prompt already includes the core writing rules.

## The short version

If you only remember five things:

1. Vary your sentence length. Let one run long, then cut it short.
2. Stop writing in threes.
3. If you could swap in another brand's name and the sentence still works, rewrite it.
4. Never leave a single word on its own line.
5. Don't let every slide look the same. Mix the alignment, break the grid once, and put a real photo on the cover.
